How it works

A ranking engine, not a newsletter.

Every stock on the exchange is scored against the same rules, every day. Nothing is hand-picked, and the list doesn't care what anyone on TV thinks.

Scored, then ranked

Each name gets a composite score from price behaviour, trend structure and volume. The ranking is the output — you always see where a stock sits relative to everything else.

Three conviction tiers

Names sort into Strong Buy, Buy and Watch. The tier tells you how much of the engine's criteria a stock currently satisfies, so you can size your attention accordingly.

Rank history you can check

See how long a name has held its position and whether it's climbing or slipping. A stock that's been top-ranked for weeks reads very differently from one that appeared yesterday.

Sector rotation at a glance

A sector summary shows where the ranked names are clustering, so you can see which parts of the market money is actually rotating into.

A journal that stays honest

Log your entries and exits against the signals you acted on. Over time it tells you something no backtest can: how you actually traded the list.

Same rules, every session

The engine doesn't get bored, doesn't chase headlines and doesn't change its mind after a bad week. Consistency is the whole point.
